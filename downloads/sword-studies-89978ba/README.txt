BARTGAME — five sword-swing studies

These are selectable comparison prototypes, not a chosen production update.
The released default game remains byte-identical to the native art reboot.
Open any numbered .gba in mGBA. Its number matches the online review gallery.

1 Planted slash: fast, restrained, 16 updates.
2 Broad sweep: larger body turn and longer forward catch, 20 updates.
3 Step-and-cut: forward body/root weight transfer, 18 updates.
4 Overhand chop: deliberate overhead load and compression, 22 updates.
5 Flowing backhand: reverse arc with an expressive return, 18 updates.

A new game's sword starts on B. D-pad walks, R rolls/lifts/throws, Start opens
the inventory/map, and A/B/L use assigned items. Compare the same enemy and
distance in each build. The titles are the same; distinguish builds by filename.
Only the opt-in sword gesture, visible reach, damage timing and swish onset vary.
Damage strength, movement, enemies and other item actions stay the same.
The drawn step is cosmetic weight transfer, not a physical world lunge.

Actual mGBA video, individual/slow/hitbox views, research and critique:
