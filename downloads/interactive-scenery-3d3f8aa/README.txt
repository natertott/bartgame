BARTGAME: readable interactive scenery

Open bartgame-interactive-scenery-3d3f8aa.gba in mGBA or a compatible GBA emulator.

Larger wooden, brass and relic chests; full-tile switches; cave/hollow/thicket
entrances taller than Bloom; clear pits, cracked barriers, cobwebs, locks,
censers, pots and healing shrines. Loot stays behind intact pocket barriers.
A pre-existing unreachable-censer placement bug is fixed.

Existing rewards, progression and save data remain compatible. Previous
forest, blight, Bloom, item and opening-screen updates are included.
