# Weathered Heights review ROM

Native GBA review build, Cream profile12. Source f4b917f7964941d488c4d20fce073f3f58b950bc.

Enter The Curdled Cream through its ladder in the Drain. New physical terrain, selected Weathered Curdstone, carved ascents and animated blue falls are active in this ROM. Other regions and current character/item art retain their selected profiles.

Pause with Start. Press R twice from the main Items page to reach the Cream map. L cycles ten concepts, Select cycles backward, A zooms the selected room, B returns, R opens items and Start resumes play. The ten maps are review alternatives; the first is the initial layout.

The gallery distinguishes actual pause-menu screenshots and ordinary gameplay clips from stitched frozen-renderer room surveys. Seeded room-load and hero-navigation capture fixtures are disclosed. A normal new run chooses its own map.
