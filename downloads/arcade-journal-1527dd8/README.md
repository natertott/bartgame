Open bartgame-1527dd8.gba in mGBA. This is the main game, with normal inventory,
progression and saves; the three new enemies join ordinary forest encounters.
The original forest-art songs play on the title, in the Drain/forest/Heart
fight and at victory. Original source and playback provenance are documented.

The retro cabinet stands on the north bank beside the wizard/storage.
Face it from the front and pressA. L/R changes Items/Enemies, D-pad browses,
A opens notes, B backs out/leaves, Start leaves. Only discovered entries reveal
names, images and information. Discoveries persist across runs in a separate
profile; run-save v1/v2 and independent appearance settings remain compatible.
To continue an existing emulator save, copy its .sav alongside this ROM with
the matching basename bartgame-1527dd8.sav.
