## Visual judgment

The supplied Emerald crop emphasizes dense, overlapping leaf crowns, a darker
hanging skirt, short supporting trunks and ordinary grass around their tips.
The new revision adopts that construction in original native pixels. It is a
material improvement over the exposed branching diagrams on purple-black
panels. Healthy canopy now reads as a leafy forest mass; infected canopy keeps
that volume, with gray/olive sickness, surviving green groups and attached
violet wounds. The large piece has unequal overlapping crowns rather than a
regular six-cap orchard. Compact fragments and shallow strips have their own
crown/thicket forms.

Two actual-room problems were caught after the first source review. Cropping
large crowns at the native canvas boundary produced ruler-straight carpet
edges; whole hanging skirts now fit within the canvas. A generic floor-type
check also allowed the inherited brown path fallback beneath healthy forest
interiors. The canopy now preserves valid stored/local floors and falls back
to stage-appropriate grass. Genuine stored or adjacent paths remain valid.

All five natural geometry cases were inspected at native size, followed by the
final healthy/deep room views and walk/roll sequence. The flat purple-black
field and invented brown fallback are gone. Crown tips meet actual grass or
sick ground, and Bloom remains discernible beneath projected foliage.

Remaining limitations are visible: a few grid-aligned joins and short repeated
leaf bands survive; healthy leaf texture is fairly busy; gray/olive infected
crowns can suggest mossy stone at a glance. The finite native kit recurs.
These are candid review caveats, not a claim of a completely hand-composed or
finished forest. No further broad art redraw is recommended before user review.

branch map and four authored tree groups match. Existing non-canopy ground,
Bloom/equipment/selected enemies, items, interfaces, Drain art and physical
metadata retain their prior payloads. Every full historical profile 0–28 ROM
matches its prior frozen bytes. Logical room-generation source is unchanged.

The native catalog has fourteen source pieces and one exact palette-inspection
GIF. Its 52-file manifest, native P16 indices, healthy/deep RGBA pixels,
literal catalog panels and four-depth/pulse panels are independently checked.
They are labelled source artwork rather than emulator screenshots.

Fresh actual runs cover sixteen contexts: eight matched profile 19 controls and
eight new profile 29 cases, including five generated geometries, walking/rolling,
cape use and one focused menu round trip. The producer records **420/420**
capture checks, 800 frame observations, four videos and four exact-color GIFs.
Independent checking retains strict physical, input, progress and action-RNG
comparisons after the disclosed post-generation starting fixture.

For every new canopy cell, raw stored-floor and logical-neighbor IDs are used
to recompute the intended local grass/soil material independently. Actual BG2
quarters must match that native ROM floor. Every sampled hardware observation
binds the complete per-case cell snapshot. Every placed BG1 quarter is checked,
including transparent tile-zero writes, so an old backing cannot survive an
intended alpha gap. Dense leaf ink and passing counters alone are not used as
an aesthetic verdict.

The current Bloom/body fragment remains a native subset, and a cape counts
only when its native phase, palette and anchor are exact. Minimum displayed
character coverage is 37 colored pixels with a 14-pixel connected component in
these samples. Ordinary-input cadence matches; no universal animation,
all-seed visibility or CPU guarantee is asserted.

compared with 47 in previous adaptive profile 19, with the same two gameplay
updates. The focused menu case resumes at tick 49 versus tick 50 in profile 19.
These timings are reported as observations; identical absolute menu handoff
was not asserted. Recorded playing intervals remain one update per IRQ except
for the same declared legacy interface-entry intervals.

Forest residency is 986/1,024 background subtiles and 11/12 world banks.
Linked use is 234,772 EWRAM bytes and 23,220 IWRAM bytes. The conservative OBJ
union is 864/876 tiles. These bounds and finite recordings support the review
build; they do not establish every-room performance. Source diagrams and
resident BG1/BG2 layer reconstructions remain distinct from native actual
