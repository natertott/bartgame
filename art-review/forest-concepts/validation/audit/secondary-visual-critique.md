# Second independent forest readability critique

Reviewed native source evidence associated with art freeze `73df5c1296f84ac469102c92f106dc9ad17b9a27`. These sheets are **source drawings and assemblies, not mGBA captures**. No new art, engine, build or test changes were made for this review.

The five paired forest samples communicate open ground and solid foliage clearly. Green mid-value soil and the continuous warm dirt path stay quieter than the raised dark canopy. Deep variants preserve that forest identity while changing tree structure, bare limbs and attached growth. Willow's hanging forms and cedar's layered triangular crowns provide the strongest silhouette changes; oak, beech and orchard deliberately remain a closer rounded-crown family.

The interaction vocabulary is substantially easier to distinguish from permanent scenery. Whole stones have broad uninterrupted light planes; bombable stone has a large pale branching break. The web reads as connected fibres. Gold gate bars and a central keyhole are separate from the dark wall texture. Switches occupy a full tile, and the pressed face changes both value and symbol. Chests have a clear lid/latch/contact edge; the relic tier is especially distinct. Cave, hollow and thicket entrances have large dark apertures, visible thresholds and different enclosing materials. The door opening reads as a destination rather than a decoration.

The character sheets share a compact body scale, warm-lit faces and few clothing masses. Hood, veil and the gardener's headwear/tool continue into the portraits. Bright treasure and pale stone stand out against the sampled ground. The provided terrain sheets do not include the selected enemies or moving pickups, so they cannot establish those actors' live contrast or occlusion; that belongs to the separate actual capture review.

Tradeoffs worth retaining in the comparison: dense repeated canopy boundaries still expose a regular band, particularly cedar. Small rock versus heavy boulder relies mainly on footprint size; the cracked bomb barrier is the stronger tool cue. Wooden versus brass chest tiers are subtler at1× than the relic tier. The unlit censer can initially read as a low bowl or stool until its lit counterpart or interaction is seen. Thicket framing is the entrance most likely to merge with dense leaves, although its black aperture and bright threshold help. These are useful next-pass targets after a direction is chosen, rather than reasons to replace the whole vocabulary.

No material blocker is apparent in these source views. This is a bounded visual judgment, not a user preference decision, runtime success claim or approval of every possible generated composition. Native palette/ID/physics preservation is established by the parent's separate independent ROM audit.
