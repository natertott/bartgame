# Selected enemy set: independent visual review

Reviewed actual mGBA chronological filmstrips for all five enemy families, the clear-floor Coin Toad encounter, the cave group, the boss attack phases and the real sword defeat; cross-checked all thirteen Heart poses with actual screenshot pixels and OAM against the frozen ROM.

The Coin Toad keeps a low, wide travelling silhouette with a clear compact turn/jump profile; the extra clear-floor encounter proves the body remains intact away from terrain occlusion. The Quill Moth alternates open and closed wings and uses a narrow forward silhouette for its pursuit phase. The Bellflower keeps the directional bloom visible above its base and its pale seed remains distinguishable while travelling. The Pear Bladder shows a flatter preparation pose followed by a tall airborne pear, with the actual vertical motion kept separate from its shadow. The Confessor remains a tall, pale folded-wing silhouette; its independently drawn weapon follows the same established combat and menu-return behavior.

The Heart remains recognizable as two purple lobes separated by a jagged central split throughout all thirteen poses. The pulse expands the lobes modestly, the hurt pose catches a brighter highlight, release and spawn poses move the cleft, and the lunge compresses its profile while the actual actor moves. At native scale those attack-pose differences are restrained; existing projectile and displacement cues carry much of the timing signal. No mouth or substitute creature silhouette replaces the broken heart.

The death uses two visibly smaller, darker, split-lobe poses. In the actual defeat these alternate every eight update ticks over the existing death interval, producing repeated convulsions before removal and the existing ending screen. This is a contraction cycle, not a progressive dissolve or a newly timed death sequence. The first few defeat frames can blink under the existing hurt/invulnerability behavior; subsequent visible contraction frames show intact split lobes without stray detached art. The final removal and ending transition succeed.

All selected source/native sheets, companions and material palettes pass the independent audit; no AI, damage timing, collision, draw ordering, gameplay fixture or preserved world/UI/art rules were changed for this integration. These recordings use the previously disclosed repeatable player/seed/phase-access fixtures and real enemy AI.
