## Candid limits

Dense interiors still have a regular32px rhythm: central green crown planes repeat across a dark/purple matrix. Edge trees are more convincing than a wall filled entirely by the interior motif. Isolated1×1pieces read as branch/crown fragments; they do not communicate the complete anatomy of a mature standalone tree. These should be presented as eight reviewable canopy treatments, rather than as a claim that every forest composition is finished. Further silhouette/parity variation can follow the user's chosen direction; generation/layout was deliberately held for this comparison. Existing modest overhang preserves paths and interactions but creates shallower depth than a large canopy spanning several ground tiles.

Broad horizontal branches follow the user's current direction. Richard Janes's cited introductory guidance supports controlled pixel-line runs and readable shapes; it does not establish a universal law that horizontal strokes are always better than vertical ones. No reference artwork pixels were copied.
