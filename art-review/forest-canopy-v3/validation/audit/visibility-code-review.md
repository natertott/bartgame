Reviewed optimized frozen game/art commit `e923557aa74b01626f3e599f7c4b50366e096507`. Exact source/review-tool digests are in `visibility-reviewed-inputs.json`. This supersedes the archived runtime-scan candidate review; that candidate had a measured update-cadence regression and is not the delivered ROM.

The asset builder calculates all 133 native hero-frame lower-band rows offline. The five values are cutoff, total opaque, lower opaque, total meaningful-colored and lower meaningful-colored pixels. Meaningful color means nonzero ink and RGB555 max channel at least 5 and channel sum at least 10. The independently packed-ROM audit verifies exact counts, minimal satisfying cutoff, source fraction, and four-connected colored component. Targets are at least one third of the original opaque/colored source, at least 48 opaque/24 colored where the source has them, and a 12-colored-pixel component where possible. F_HERO_FALL_3 originally contains only four meaningful pixels in two disconnected pairs; it keeps its entire unchanged source, explicitly not an invented 12-pixel cluster.

At draw time the body remains behind BG1. Only exact existing current-pose pixels inside the protected lower band AND under actual opaque vegetation-overlay pixels are copied into a reserved masked OBJ. The mask uses the same anchor, horizontal flip, palette and fall/jump origin as that body. The renderer excludes timber-arch roofs and non-forest scenery. Hurt blinking, hidden actors and intentional terminal falling/death invisibility retain their established behavior. This is a limited pixel-visibility convention for foliage, not translucent foliage or a universal sorting system.

The reserved body-plus-mask VRAM packet flushes after the ordinary 16-entry queue. It cannot be silently dropped when that ordinary queue fills, and refreshes both the current body and its own current mask. When a mask is required, directionally front-held parts retain front OAM ordering and temporarily use priority1; north/far-side parts retain their previous priority2. The full front tool may therefore show in front of a few leaves during the protected interval. No hero/tool art, combat timings, reach or collision changes.

The actual-frame verifier now uses explicit noncontiguous native HeroCape IDs, matching the current native FX phase/palette and exact same OAM anchor/flip/shape/size. Matching an idle/cape anticipation body bitmap alone is insufficient. Only a proven attached current cape joins the displayed character; sword/effects/UI/nearby actors cannot count toward visibility. Body-versus-canopy proof remains separate at one quarter of colored native body and at least12 meaningful pixels/component8. A cape can cover body colors while the complete Bloom character remains visible.
