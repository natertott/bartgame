# Independent native review: Drain v3

Reviewed against the user's v2 feedback, not against arbitrary image-difference scores.
These observations concern authored source pixels. Actual mGBA motion/occlusion is a separate pass.

## Earlier sketches rejected or refined

- V2 waterfall: two thin, continuous meandering green strokes read as roots. V3 changes the actual construction to broad gravity-aligned liquid sheets, separated falling highlight packets, a spreading foam impact and sparse concentric pool ripples.
- First v3 floor #2 sketch lost recognizable bricks; the revised running bond restores coherent masses.
- First v3 #3 floor had unbroken vertical joins that resembled upright wall panels. The revised joints interrupt and stagger.
- Dense #5 herringbone remains intentionally denser than the other four. Its joint contrast was reduced after review; it should be offered with that tradeoff, not declared universally superior.
- The first v3 stream draft still repeated short marks along the same three rows. Varied vertical offsets and quieter gaps reduce the stripe/fabric reading.
- The first ladder sign was high enough to sit beneath the fixed HUD. The final arrow sign is at world y32–46 beside the ladder lane; actual northern-camera review still needs to confirm it is visible in gameplay.

## Final fixture/NPC sketches

- The final ladder sign is a plain ivory up arrow on a dark rectangular field, clearly separated from the bright rails and arch.
- The broad arch and circular catch basin keep the liked #1/#4 language. The final source water sheets and terminal pale foam read as water at native scale; they no longer branch or taper like roots.
- The five frogs retain the preferred broad-eyed, broad-jawed #1 identity with distinct expressions/proportions/apron choices. The wizard family retains the preferred #5 pointed purple hidden-face cowl and separate flame-tipped staff. Portraits carry the same identities with more information.
- The original preferred #1 frog body/pose construction is deliberately retained where useful. Preserved baseline artwork and repeated blank/seam fragments are not failures of coverage and should not receive meaningless pixels merely to force new hashes.
- Crate tops now visibly consist of wood boards and lid braces. Attached grime belongs on the surrounding masonry and lower contact areas, rather than as an unexplained green/gray cap hiding the wood.

Full-room, fixture, grayscale and chronological source-animation checks follow gallery generation. Real-run source hashes, budgets and limitations are reported separately.

## Map-alias problems caught by the complete assembled fixture pass

The first supposedly finished v3 source assembly still discarded the small
outlet's brass riser and black mouth, leaving only an unexplained drip. The
code painted them, but the repeated floor-ID substitution erased the pixels.
This was a presentation defect despite legal source frames and passing
encoding checks. Final publication was paused and the pilot was archived.

The corrected outlet uses unique bank-face tiles for a plainly round brass
mouth and short wall-connected stub, followed by the unique animated stream
tile for the fall, impact foam and ripples. No physical room or collision
metadata changed; no extra OBJ or tile ID was needed. Its full-map proof now
makes the plumbing's purpose obvious. Gallery crops were widened to include
the connection to the side wall.

The same pass exposed gaps in the horizontal north-wall brass pipe. Root
restricted the special crop to existing swh_058/059, whose occurrences belong
to that same pipe, rather than replacing them with generic wall material. The
final pipe is connected without duplicating plumbing into unrelated floor
cells. The complete source gallery is regenerated from the corrected freeze;
none of the archived pilot captures should be published as final evidence.
