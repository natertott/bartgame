# Five native Cream polish studies

The user's second Cream review requests five finer wall treatments, a stronger
precipice read, shade connected to the wall, many cleaner ladder iterations,
and blue waterfalls of varying width. These are optional profiles7–11;0–6
remain historical choices. No new global palette/default is selected.

Each wall kit has seven native32/64/96px complete pictures and a128×48 quiet
backing at three corruption stages. Explicit exterior masks preserve the
background instead of erasing it with a rectangular matte. Internal shadow
ink remains inside each silhouette. BG-ground composition retains Bloom's
existing priority; the art never becomes a semantic overlay/collider. A whole
panel must fit after all regional mechanisms and furniture are present and
must fit the finite reserved-character budget. Rejecting a complete panel is
preferable to cutting off a branch or fracture to fill a little gap.

The15 ladder studies are authored at32px width with coherent top/middle/foot
segments. Repeated middle sections join actual paired columns with two main
rails or one deliberate handhold pattern. Floor-bank feet avoid dark pasted
rectangles. All retain the actual existing climb collision and entry geometry.

Actual jumpable sills gain a shallow visible projection and a darker solid
underside. Raised borders still block movement. Shade follows supported floor
from a real cliff base to the actual incoming-drop landing, including paths.
It must not be an isolated puddle/oval, obscure an interaction, or suggest a
platform that physics does not support.

Blue falls have native top, moving sheet and foaming foot compositions at
16/32/48px widths. Four regularly advancing phases replace the older two-pose
flow. Only complete measured unused cliff intervals may host them. No extra
interior border splits a two/three-wide sheet; no dry route becomes harmful
water. Existing blue-water pools use the same legal sixteen-color bank.

Native16-color RGB555 assets, masks, geometry/role checks and actual mGBA
captures qualify these examples. Source sheets are not emulator footage;
registered full-room mosaics are not one240×160 live framebuffer. The read
[reference note](CRENEL_REFERENCE.md), [shared direction](../art-reboot/ART_BIBLE.md)
and [prior refinement](FRACTURED_RIND.md) supply context. The published review
will record exact source/ROM identities, native budgets and finite test limits.
