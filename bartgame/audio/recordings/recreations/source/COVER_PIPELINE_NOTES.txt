AUTOMATIC INSTRUMENTAL GAME COVERS

These are note/transient-based instrumental reconstructions. They are not exact,
human-verified transcriptions. The original mix comparison, if supplied alongside
them, must remain clearly identified as a separate playback of the source mix.

The cover synthesis accepts symbolic events only. No uploaded waveform, vocal,
audio sample or extracted source stem is mixed into the cover PCM. Instrument
sounds come from the FluidR3 GM SoundFont; the game subsequently plays their
offline stereo mix through its Direct Sound PCM channels. Vocal pitch candidates
may therefore be played as instrumental notes, with no recreated sung words.

The pitch-input owner runs Spotify Basic Pitch locally on full-mix, harmonic,
bass-band and stereo-side analysis views. HPSS is a harmonic/percussive signal
decomposition, not vocal or instrument isolation. The selected HPSS view supplies
nonbass candidates; a coherent separate bass-band pass supplies bass candidates.
The engine does not blindly sum all model passes. Stereo-side alternatives are
retained for review but are not currently added, because they can overdetect
harmonic/octave notes. Model activation is salience, not calibrated correctness.

Native source timestamps are preserved, with no new tempo grid, song rewrite,
lead-in trimming, fade removal or shortened musical form. MIDI/score BPM120 is
only a seconds-to-beats export timebase: one beat is0.5seconds. The final cover
duration equals the exact decoded-source duration from source_manifest.json,
rounded only to the nearest output sample. The GBA build may append tiny frame
alignment padding separately. A full-length file can still contain missed or
incorrect automatically detected notes; duration is not proof of transcription
completeness.

Among overlapping bass candidates, model salience selects one active note. An
independent low-band YIN trace may break a tie only when its valid periodic F0
matches an already-existing note plus bend within0.85semitones. It creates no new
notes or timings. Genuine raw sub fundamentals21..27 are kept: Direct Sound PCM
has no PSG minimum-pitch restriction. A source01 pilot independently supported
the B0/C1 region. Other octave-estimation errors remain possible.

Basic Pitch bend trajectories are interpreted as1/3semitone bins and applied to
the monophonic bass at20ms control intervals. They are also exported as MIDI
pitchwheel messages with a12-semitone wheel range. Other voices use their detected
equal-tempered note centers. A salience-weighted nonoverlapping foreground path
is assigned a prominent instrument; remaining original-time notes are retained
in counter/harmony instruments. This assignment does not identify original stems.

Drum candidates come from full-source HPSS-masked low/mid/high spectral flux,
with local adaptive thresholds, conservative salience gates and approximately
9.98ms timing resolution. These transients are re-created with fresh GM kick,
snare/clap and hat sounds. No artificial metronome, tempo snapping or missing-beat
fill is added. Vocal consonants, bass attacks and plucks can be false detections;
quiet or overlapping drums can be missed. The class labels are heuristic.

cover_engine.py can rebuild a saved symbolic score without reading source audio:
  python3 cover_engine.py --id 1 --score covers/01_example.cover.json \
      --midi covers/01_example.mid --render covers/01_example.npy

Use --notes, --bass-notes, --bass-f0 and a cached --drums-json to rebuild a score
from the analysis outputs. The saved score records input/model/code hashes and
selection limitations. render_covers_batch.py validates exact sample counts,
finite stereo output, monophonic foreground/bass and preserved raw sub register.

The float32 NPY/WAV files produced by this engine are PRE-GBA synthesis files.
User-facing recordings should come from actual execution of the cartridge in
GBA emulation and be labeled accordingly. No public upload is part of this code.
