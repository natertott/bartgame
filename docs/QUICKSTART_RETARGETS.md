# Retargeted transitions, and where vanilla sends them

Generated by `tools/quickstart/retarget_report.py` from `src/data/transitions.c`.
Regenerate with `python3 tools/quickstart/retarget_report.py --md`.

**Classes.** `RETARGETED` is a different destination room entirely. `BLOCKED`
is a vanilla door this build removes on purpose. `MOVED` - same room,
different landing - is empty, and so is `ADDED`.

Retiring the 2-door "? room" pool returned 19 rooms' doors to vanilla and cut
RETARGETED from 65 to 27. Retiring the SINGLE-door pool's retargets took it
from 27 to 6 - twenty-one rooms whose only door sent the player to Castle
Garden Main (three dojos, three tree hollows, ten Minish house interiors,
Gina's grave, the Veil Falls heart-piece cave, the Minish Woods Great Fairy)
now open where they really open. Melari's Mine's door went back to vanilla
too, taking it to 5.

**Every one of the five that remain is the same thing:** the mode has no
Hyrule Town, so the four field rooms that border it are stitched to each
other instead, and Stockwell's shop - a Hyrule Town interior - opens into
North Hyrule Field. Nothing is left over from any retired feature.

Two places worth naming because they have been asked about and are NOT in
the table: **Castle Garden's south border** (vanilla; it pointed at Melari's
Mine in the hub era and has not for a long time) and **Melari's Mine itself**
(vanilla in both directions, including its three side-room doors - the
trigger boxes that used to shadow those three were retired from
`sQuickStartLinks`, so the doors are unopposed).
